iPhone Safari can't make a web page fullscreen, but you can install Feluna as an app:

  1. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ in Safari
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Feluna from your home screen

It opens without Safari's bars. Note: the home screen app keeps its own saved progress, separate from Safari's.
